[Main Theme - The Feather Theme]
[the famous melody enters way up high on tiptoes, delicate and ringing, an octave above where you expect it]
[hook bar one: a chain of even eighth notes climbing stepwise with a gentle repeated-note lilt, like the feather lifting off the ground]
[hook bar two: the line crests on its highest note with a small leap, then drifts back down and settles into a dotted rhythm, gliding not flapping]
[hook bar three: the climb begins again and reaches one step higher]
[hook bar four: the phrase lands on a long held whole note, letting everybody look at her]
[the theme restates an octave lower, dressed in warm parallel thirds, mezzo-piano, feet finally touching the grass]

[Brighter Statement - lightly]
[key shifts brighter, forte but light on its feet, the melody now carrying a bouncing countermelody in the left hand]
[the theme climbs higher than before, phrases keep breathing, a polite pause after each one, big but never sweaty]
[settles softly, mezzo-piano diminuendo, a fermata held like a caught breath]

[Passionate Section - in three]
[time turns to a gentle waltz, full romantic warmth opens its chest, forte]
[big rolled chords in the left hand, melody surging in little triplet figures, soaring heartfelt phrases reaching up like arms, everybody in the theater crying]
[swells rise and ease, forte to mezzo-piano and back, unashamed and sincere]

[Simple Section - semplice, in two]
[everything falls away to bare skipping piano, plain as bread and twice as sweet, mezzo-piano]

[the little strain rises an octave, ritardando e diminuendo, fading to a fermata]

[Dissolve - slowly, freely]
[time melts, quiet held notes drifting over a deep sustained bass, soft triplet curls floating free of the bar lines, nobody rushes her]

[Build]

[Finale - rhythmically, with excitement]
[she runs, driving accented triplet momentum, mezzo-forte to forte, accents popping like flashbulbs, joyful sprint too fast for the bar lines]
